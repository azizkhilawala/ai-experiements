Connect your calendar

Weave your real work between meetings.

Weft reads your calendar, finds the open stretches, and threads your priorities through them in 25 or 60 minute blocks. When a meeting lands on a block, Weft moves the block instead of dropping it.

Connect your calendar Free for one calendar

How it works

  1. Connect your calendar

    Google Calendar, Outlook, or iCloud. Your meetings stay exactly where they are, and Weft plans around them.

  2. Name this week’s priority

    Pick one thing. Weft gives it the longest open stretches first, then fits your other tasks into whatever is left.

  3. Work the plan

    Blocks show up on your calendar as busy. If someone books over one, Weft moves it to the next gap that fits and tells you where it went.

Every Friday, a swatch of your week

Weft keeps each finished week as a small piece of cloth. Line up a month of them and you’ll see patterns a calendar hides.

  • Long bands

    Week of Aug 31

    The priority got 18 hours, most of it before lunch.

  • Mostly barcode

    Week of Sep 7

    Meetings filled most mornings. Weft found three recurring ones that could move to the afternoon.

  • Short and scattered

    Week of Sep 14

    No focus block lasted longer than 25 minutes. Weft suggests batching small tasks into one hour.

  • Mostly green

    Week of Sep 21

    Thursday and Friday off, and the priority still got five hours on Monday and Tuesday.

What Weft handles for you

Moves blocks instead of deleting them
A new meeting on top of a focus block pushes the block to the next open gap that fits. You get one short note saying where it landed.
Sizes blocks to the task
Small tasks get 25 minutes. Anything that needs real thinking gets an hour, or several hours in a row when your calendar allows it.
Keeps task names private
Coworkers see your blocks as busy. What you’re working on stays between you and Weft.
Works with the calendars you have
Google Calendar, Outlook, and iCloud, with apps for Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android.
